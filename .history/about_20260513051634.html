Our Story

HOW WE GOT HERE

From a single van and two technicians, to Boston's most trusted pest control company. Here's the journey.

2009 — Founded
One Van, One Mission
Edward O'Malley and his brother Thomas started LABS PEST CONTROL out of a Boston garage with a secondhand sprayer and a deep conviction that homeowners deserved honest, professional pest control — not a quick fix.
2012 — Certification
Officially Licensed & SON Certified
After three years of growth, LABS PEST CONTROL earned full QualityPro certification and EPA-aligned safety accreditation, becoming one of only a handful of licensed operators in the Commonwealth.
2015 — Expansion
Commercial Services Launch
Demand from hotels, hospitals, and schools led us to launch a dedicated commercial division. By year-end we were protecting 40+ businesses including a major Boston hospital network.
2018 — Innovation
Eco-Safe Treatment Line
We partnered with international researchers to introduce Nigeria's first child-safe, low-toxicity fumigation protocol — eliminating pests without exposing families to harsh chemical residue.
2023 — Scale
10,000 Homes Milestone
LABS PEST CONTROL crossed 10,000 treated properties across Greater Boston. Our team of 60+ certified technicians now covers every major neighborhood, with a 24-hour emergency response line operating seven days a week.
Today
Boston's Most Trusted Pest Control
Over 14,000 protected properties, a 98% client satisfaction rate, and a team that genuinely cares about every home we enter. The mission hasn't changed — only the scale.

How We Work

OUR PROCESS

No guesswork. No upselling. Just a clear, methodical process that actually eliminates pests.

01
Inspection
A certified technician conducts a thorough inspection of your property — including walls, roof cavities, plumbing, and storage areas — to identify pest type, severity, and entry points.
Free for new clients
02
Custom Plan
We design a treatment plan specific to your infestation, home layout, and household needs — including whether you have children, pets, or medical sensitivities.
No one-size-fits-all
03
Treatment
Our technicians apply eco-safe, targeted treatments at the source — not just surfaces. We work methodically through every affected zone to ensure full elimination.
Eco-safe products
04
Follow-Up
We return 2–4 weeks post-treatment to verify elimination. If pests return within 90 days, we re-treat at zero additional cost — no questions asked.
90-day guarantee
